# COOKIE POLICY

**Last Updated: October 1, 2026**

## 1. INTRODUCTION

This Cookie Policy explains how CUSIS ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website at cusis.in and our dashboard application (the "Service").

This policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

### 1.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences, understand how you use the site, and improve your experience.

### 1.2 Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use:
- **Local Storage:** Browser storage for larger amounts of data
- **Session Storage:** Temporary storage that expires when you close your browser
- **Web Beacons (Pixels):** Small graphic images that track page visits
- **SDKs:** Software development kits in mobile applications

For simplicity, we refer to all these technologies as "cookies" in this policy.

## 2. WHY WE USE COOKIES

We use cookies to:
- **Enable essential Service functionality** - Authentication, security, and core features
- **Remember your preferences** - Settings, language, and customization
- **Understand how you use the Service** - Analytics and performance monitoring
- **Improve user experience** - Optimize features and interface
- **Provide security** - Prevent fraud and protect your account
- **Enhance ORDIS AI** - Maintain context for better assistance

## 3. TYPES OF COOKIES WE USE

### 3.1 Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are necessary for the Service to function and cannot be disabled.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|------------|---------|----------|------|
| `session_id` | Authenticates your login session | Session | First-party |
| `csrf_token` | Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks | Session | First-party |
| `auth_token` | Maintains secure authentication | 30 days | First-party |
| `security_check` | Verifies security status | 24 hours | First-party |

**What happens if you block these:**
The Service will not function properly. You will not be able to log in or access your account.

### 3.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|------------|---------|----------|------|
| `user_preferences` | Stores your settings and preferences | 1 year | First-party |
| `language` | Remembers your language selection | 1 year | First-party |
| `theme` | Stores your theme preference (light/dark) | 1 year | First-party |
| `sidebar_state` | Remembers sidebar expand/collapse state | 30 days | First-party |
| `view_mode` | Remembers your preferred view (list/grid) | 90 days | First-party |
| `timezone` | Stores your timezone for correct time display | 1 year | First-party |

**What happens if you block these:**
The Service will still work, but you'll need to reset your preferences each time you visit.

### 3.3 Analytics and Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the Service so we can improve it.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|------------|---------|----------|------|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics - Distinguishes users | 2 years | Third-party |
| `_gid` | Google Analytics - Distinguishes users | 24 hours | Third-party |
| `_gat` | Google Analytics - Throttles request rate | 1 minute | Third-party |
| `analytics_session` | Tracks usage patterns | Session | First-party |
| `feature_usage` | Monitors feature adoption | 90 days | First-party |
| `performance_metrics` | Measures page load times | 30 days | First-party |

**Information collected:**
- Pages you visit
- Features you use
- Time spent on pages
- Click patterns
- Error occurrences
- Device and browser information

**What happens if you block these:**
The Service will work normally. We won't be able to understand your usage patterns to improve the Service.

### 3.4 ORDIS AI Context Cookies

These cookies help ORDIS AI provide better assistance by maintaining conversation context.

| Cookie Name | Purpose | Duration | Type |
|------------|---------|----------|------|
| `ordis_session` | Maintains AI conversation context | 24 hours | First-party |
| `ordis_preferences` | Remembers your AI interaction preferences | 90 days | First-party |
| `ai_context` | Stores recent workspace actions for AI | Session | First-party |

**What happens if you block these:**
ORDIS AI will still work but may provide less personalized assistance.

### 3.5 Marketing and Advertising Cookies (If Applicable)

Currently, we do NOT use marketing or advertising cookies. If this changes:
- We will update this policy
- We will obtain your explicit consent

## 5. HOW TO MANAGE COOKIES

### 5.1 Cookie Consent Manager

When you first visit CUSIS, you'll see a cookie consent banner allowing you to:
- Accept all cookies
- Reject non-essential cookies
- Customize your cookie preferences

You can change your preferences anytime by:
- Clicking the cookie settings link in the footer
- Accessing Cookie Preferences in your account settings
- Emailing support@cusis.in

### 5.2 Browser Controls

All modern browsers allow you to control cookies through settings:

**Google Chrome:**
1. Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
2. Choose your preferred option
3. Manage exceptions for specific sites

**Mozilla Firefox:**
1. Settings → Privacy & Security
2. Under Cookies and Site Data, adjust settings
3. Manage individual site permissions

**Safari:**
1. Preferences → Privacy
2. Adjust cookie settings
3. Manage website data

**Microsoft Edge:**
1. Settings → Cookies and site permissions
2. Choose cookie preferences
3. Manage or delete cookies

**Mobile Browsers:**
Check your mobile browser's settings menu for cookie controls.

### 5.3 Clearing Cookies

To delete existing cookies:
1. Access your browser's settings
2. Find "Clear browsing data" or similar
3. Select cookies and site data
4. Choose time range and confirm

**Note:** Clearing cookies will log you out and reset your preferences.

### 5.4 Browser Plugins

Browser extensions for cookie management:
- Privacy Badger (blocks tracking cookies)
- uBlock Origin (blocks ads and trackers)
- Cookie AutoDelete (automatically clears cookies)

### 5.5 Opt-Out Links

**Google Analytics:**
Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on:
https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

**Other Services:**
See Section 4.2 for third-party service opt-out options.

### 6.1 Essential Cookies Blocked

If you block functional cookies:

### 6.3 Analytics Cookies Blocked

### 6.4 ORDIS AI Cookies Blocked

If you block ORDIS AI cookies:

- ❌ Less personalized responses
- ❌ AI may ask repeated questions
- ❌ Reduced context awareness

## 8. DO NOT TRACK SIGNALS

### 8.1 Current Stance

Currently, we do not respond to "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser signals because:
- There is no universal standard for DNT
- Different browsers implement DNT differently
- No industry consensus on how to respond

### 8.2 Alternative Controls

Instead of DNT, please use:
- Our cookie consent manager
- Browser cookie controls (Section 5.2)
- Third-party opt-out tools

We monitor developments in DNT standards and may implement support in the future.
