## Why do daily status standups fail remote teams?

Remote startups and agencies often lose 30 to 45 minutes every morning repeating what each person plans to touch today—followed by interruptions in chat all afternoon whenever managers wonder whether someone is busy or finished. Status calls exist only because daily priorities and live focus state are invisible.

## The My Day 3-5 task focus cap

Cursis **My Day** (included on Pro and Business plans) solves daily over-commitment with a deliberate constraint: each teammate selects **3 to 5 tasks** for today's focus list rather than dragging 20 overdue backlog items into a single day.

- **Morning plan in 2 minutes:** Pick up to 5 assigned tasks and adjust today's hour estimate (`0.5h` increments up to an 8-hour target).
- **One active focus timer:** Start working on a single task at a time so logged hours accrue automatically on the task.
- **Clear completion tracking:** Checked tasks stay credited toward the day's total hours without resetting work capacity.

## How Team Pulse gives leads instant clarity without pings

Every commitment and active focus timer inside **My Day** updates **Team Pulse** automatically for workspace owners, admins, and managers:

- Teammates see at a glance who is currently focused on a specific deliverable and its estimated finish time.
- Managers see each person's committed hours (`Available` under 7h, `Full` at 7–8h, `Overloaded` above 8h) before assigning urgent requests.
- Cross-workspace guards ensure only one focus timer runs at a time, preventing accidental double-counting across client workspaces.

When daily intent, active work, and remaining capacity stay visible in one workspace, teams trade recurring status meetings for uninterrupted deep work.
