## What does tool sprawl actually cost a 6-to-10-person team?

Most small startups and agencies run the same fragmented stack: one subscription for team chat, another for project boards, a third for meeting scheduling and notes, and a fourth AI assistant with zero awareness of workspace deadlines. Beyond paying multiple per-seat bills every month, teammates lose hours reconstructing context whenever a decision happens in chat and fails to update a task board.

## Flat workspace pricing vs. per-seat SaaS stacking

Cursis consolidates grouped task streams, drag-and-drop Kanban boards, project tracking, meeting notes, calendar views, daily focus timers, and **Ordis AI** into simple flat workspace tiers:

- **Starter Plan ($9 USD/month launch pricing, regular $19):** Up to 6 team members, 10 projects, and 5 tasks per project—covering core team collaboration without per-seat surprises.
- **Pro Plan ($20 USD/month launch pricing, regular $39):** Up to 10 team members, unlimited projects, unlimited tasks, full **Ordis AI Copilot**, and the **My Day** focus planner plus live **Team Pulse**.
- **Business Plan (Custom):** Custom AI agents, bespoke dashboards, tailored automations, and dedicated onboarding for larger organizations.

Instead of multiplying separate chat, project tracker, and AI seats by 10 users, growing teams pay a predictable flat monthly rate.

## When should you keep separate tools instead?

Balanced software evaluation matters. Cursis is engineered specifically for agile 2-to-10-person teams, startups, and creator agencies that want fast clarity out of the box. Larger enterprises requiring thousands of seats across strict legacy ERP integrations or deep multi-department custom database scripting may still prefer enterprise-heavy suites alongside custom Cursis Business deployments.

## Five workflows that belong in one place

- **Turning discussions into tasks:** Attach task threads and formal approvals directly inside the deliverable instead of losing specs inside scrolling chat channels.
- **Spotting stalled work automatically:** Ask Ordis AI which tasks are overdue or blocked rather than running daily status check-ins.
- **Planning realistic workdays:** Commit to 3–5 daily tasks in **My Day** with live focus timers synced to team workload visibility.
- **Converting meetings into action:** Capture notes and turn action items into assigned sprint tasks in one step.
- **Importing existing backlogs cleanly:** Move tasks in seconds using Cursis CSV task import with row-by-row email and date validation.
