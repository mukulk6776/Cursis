## Why do agency and creator projects slip between tools?

Creative agencies and production teams rarely miss deadlines because teammates refuse to work. Deadlines slip because a client brief sits in chat, feedback lives in screenshots, and nobody owns the review gate between editing and publishing.

Instead of opening separate channels and external spreadsheets for every campaign, Cursis keeps the brief, deliverable owner, due date, file links, and explicit reviewer approval on the same task card.

## Give every client request a single home

Record the deliverable name, acceptance criteria, project association, and single primary owner before production starts. When briefs start structured, reviewers verify finished work against written scope rather than guessing from memory.

- Attach the asset or staging URL directly inside the deliverable thread.
- Group related deliverables inside a project across **List**, **Board**, **Calendar**, and **Timeline** views.
- Keep standalone tasks clearly labeled when small client fixes do not belong to a sprint.

## Plan the review step explicitly

A production deadline fails if review time is treated as zero hours. Every reviewable deliverable should specify two things before work starts: **a named reviewer** and **a target decision date**.

In Cursis, tasks with pending approvals cannot accidentally be marked completed until the designated reviewer signs off or requests changes inside the task thread. That single guardrail stops unfinished drafts from shipping.

## Make multi-role handoffs explicit across the studio

For video studios and digital agencies, work typically moves across five specialized roles:

- **Script Editor (SE):** Structures hooks, narrative flow, and spoken briefs.
- **Video Editor (VE):** Handles assembly, pacing cuts, sound design, and color pass.
- **Thumbnail Designer (TD):** Packages contrast, typography, and visual framing for CTR.
- **Social Manager (SM):** Adapts longform assets into short clips and publishing schedules.
- **Voiceover Artist (VO):** Delivers clean narration, sponsor reads, and localized audio tracks.

Before a task transitions between **To Do**, **In Progress**, **In Review**, and **Completed**, attach the latest link and summarize what decision the next owner needs.

## Finish with a repeatable bottleneck review

At project closeout, check which stage stalled longest—brief clarity, missing assets, or reviewer turnaround. Pro teams can ask **Ordis AI** to summarize delayed tasks across active projects so lessons turn into clearer checklists for the next client sprint.
