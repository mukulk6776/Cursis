# Cursis data processing addendum draft

This draft supports customer contracting. It requires the parties' verified legal identities, addresses, signature, approved sub-processor schedule, regions, security annex and retention instructions before execution.

## Parties and purposes

Customer: [legal entity and address]. Processor: Saransh Vashistha and Mukul Kumar, operating as Cursis, at [business contact address to be supplied], contact official@cursis.in. Term: service term plus the documented return/deletion period. Customer is Data Fiduciary/controller for employee, contractor, client, supplier and other individual data submitted to the workspace. Cursis processes it only to supply the contracted workspace, authorised AI features, integrations, support and security on documented instructions. Account administration and Cursis's own legal/security obligations are separately described in its Privacy Notice.

## Customer obligations

Customer establishes a valid purpose and ground, gives understandable notices, obtains consent where required, handles employment/guardian requirements and identifies legal restrictions before upload. Customer must not submit children's or specially regulated data without a separately agreed lawful workflow. The customer determines access roles, retention, authoritative correction and disclosure instructions.

## Processor obligations

Cursis limits processing to instructions unless binding law requires otherwise; where legally permitted, it informs the customer before that required processing. It flags instructions it reasonably believes unlawful. It restricts staff access, binds confidentiality, maintains access logs and reasonable safeguards, and assists with access, correction, erasure, nomination and grievances. It does not independently train models, advertise or monetise customer data under this addendum. Any different purpose needs separate lawful documentation.

## Sub-processors and transfers

Attach the approved list derived from lib/privacy/config.ts with each recipient's legal entity, function, data categories, actual processing countries, transfer basis, retention and contact. General authorisation, if chosen, requires prior written change notice and a meaningful objection procedure agreed in the order form. Bind equivalent protection by contract and retain responsibility for sub-processor performance.

Gemini and Groq routing, fallback, provider training/data-use terms and retention must both be approved before personal data is sent. Do not promise an exclusive provider while fallback remains enabled. Customer-selected integrations need specific scope and authority. No transfer may bypass Indian restrictions, stricter sector rules or applicable GDPR Chapter V requirements.

## Rights and incidents

Cursis promptly forwards rights requests to the customer and assists under agreed secure procedures; it does not reveal other tenants or grant a nominee unrestricted access. It notifies the customer of a personal data breach without undue delay, with available scope, data categories, times, safeguards, likely consequences and mitigation, followed by updates. This contract must support the customer's earliest applicable reporting deadline and does not replace Cursis's own legal reporting duties.

## Return deletion and legal retention

On lawful instructions or service termination, provide an agreed portable return and erase eligible active data. Target complete processor/backup disposition within 90 days unless an earlier duty or documented legal retention applies. Restrict retained copies, specify law and review/purge date, prevent product reuse and reapply deletion blocks after restoration. Preserve another controller's records only under that controller's independently valid duties. Supply completion evidence; a deleted MongoDB row is insufficient evidence of provider deletion.

## Assurance and security annex

Agree proportionate audit evidence and cooperation. The annex must identify production regions, access controls/MFA, tenant isolation, TLS configuration, storage and backup encryption/key management, credential vault, log retention/localisation, recovery tests, vulnerability management and incident contacts. Certifications and cryptographic claims require current evidence. Significant Data Fiduciary, GDPR representative/DPO and sector-specific duties need separate applicability assessments.

Disputes, liability, governing law and commercial precedence must be reconciled with the signed services contract by counsel.
